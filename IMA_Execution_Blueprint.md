## SECTION 1: VISION
The Independent Master Architect (IMA) is the new paradigm for the music
entrepreneur. We are transitioning from a major-label-dominated market to a
decentralized ecosystem where the artist acts as the central architect of their own
"360º independent structure".
In the legacy model, labels provided four pillars: Legal, A&R, Marketing, and
Distribution. Today, AI systems replace these high-cost roles, allowing a single
architect to manage global scale with "integrated intelligence" while retaining
100% ownership.
